Installing the chart does NOT put the proxy in the data path on its own. Two
steps remain:

1. Repoint the hubble-ui frontend's /api at this Service.

   Cilium renders the frontend's nginx.conf into the "hubble-ui-nginx" ConfigMap
   with "proxy_pass http://127.0.0.1:8090;". Replace that with:

       proxy_pass http://{{ include "hubble-authz-proxy.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }};

   then restart the hubble-ui Deployment. Note that a Cilium chart upgrade
   rewrites that ConfigMap, so this needs to be reapplied (or managed with a
   Helm post-renderer).

2. Put oauth2-proxy in front of the frontend so X-Auth-Request-* headers exist,
   and make sure it STRIPS any inbound copies of those headers from the client.
